About

Twenty-five years of building where games meet everything else.

I started in comics and licensed entertainment, moved into online worlds before social networks existed, and then spent two years putting Marvel on phones before smartphones changed the market.

Augmented reality came next, first at Ogmento, which I co-founded, and later at DAQRI. Neurable put me next to brain-computer interfaces for a couple of years, and lately it has been branded worlds on Roblox and the question of what makes people take part and come back.
