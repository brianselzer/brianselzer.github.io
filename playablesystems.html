● Playable Systems Design

Participation should change what becomes possible next.

A customer journey is usually a string of touchpoints. A playable system remembers what you did and responds, so each interaction makes the next one worth more.

Talk About a SystemHow I work with companies
The core idea

One line through every layer.

Hover or tap a layer. The dotted line is continuity: what people did on one layer shows up on the next.

Eight conditions

What strong participation needs.

  1. 01

    Agency

    People can make meaningful choices and influence what happens next.

  2. 02

    Legibility

    People understand the system, their role, and the consequences of action.

  3. 03

    Progression

    Taking part creates a sense of movement, growth, or expanding possibility.

  4. 04

    Belonging

    People feel connected to others, a shared identity, or something larger than themselves.

  5. 05

    Reciprocity

    The relationship gives something back when people invest time, attention, or effort.

  6. 06

    Contribution

    People can add something that matters to the system, community, or outcome.

  7. 07

    Recognition

    The system notices what people do and reflects their participation back to them.

  8. 08

    Continuity

    Identity, progress, context, and value persist across time and touchpoints.

Why it matters

Better participation shows up in the business.

  1. 01

    Design the conditions

    Build in agency, legibility, progression, belonging, reciprocity, contribution, recognition and continuity.

  2. 02

    Change behavior

    Give people stronger reasons to take part, return, contribute and move deeper into the relationship.

  3. 03

    Create value over time

    Turn disconnected interactions into a relationship that compounds instead of resetting at every touchpoint.

Where it applies

Products, platforms, places and worlds.

  1. A

    Entertainment and IP

    Connect stories, games, creators, communities, products and physical experiences around one audience relationship.

  2. B

    Brands and loyalty

    Move past points and promotions toward systems that recognize identity, progress and contribution.

  3. C

    Physical and digital

    Make places, products, events and screens behave like parts of one connected system.
