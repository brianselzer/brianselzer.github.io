Playable Systems Design

Participation should change what becomes possible next.

Playable Systems Design turns fragmented customer experiences into connected, responsive systems designed to grow engagement, relationships, and value over time.

It is not about turning everything into a game. It is about designing experiences that recognize people, respond to what they do, and make each interaction strengthen the next.

Talk About a System →Explore Advisory

The Core Idea

A playable system remembers what you did and responds accordingly.

Most customer journeys are collections of touchpoints. A playable system connects those touchpoints so identity, context, progress, recognition, contribution, and value can carry forward.

Connected experiences create more value when each interaction strengthens the next.

Eight Conditions

What strong participation needs

The conditions that make participation meaningful and durable.

01

Agency

People can make meaningful choices and influence what happens next.

02

Legibility

People understand the system, their role, and the consequences of action.

03

Progression

Participation creates a sense of movement, growth, or expanding possibility.

04

Belonging

People feel connected to others, a shared identity, or something larger than themselves.

05

Reciprocity

The relationship gives something back when people invest time, attention, or effort.

06

Contribution

People can add something that matters to the system, community, or outcome.

07

Recognition

The system notices what people do and reflects their participation back to them.

08

Continuity

Identity, progress, context, and value persist across time and touchpoints.

Why It Matters

Design conditions shape behavior. Behavior creates business outcomes.

The point is not engagement for its own sake. Better-designed participation can strengthen return behavior, retention, loyalty, advocacy, community, contribution, and lifetime value.

01
Design the conditions
Create agency, legibility, progression, belonging, reciprocity, contribution, recognition, and continuity.
02
Change behavior
Give people stronger reasons to participate, return, contribute, connect, and move deeper into the relationship.
03
Create value over time
Turn disconnected interactions into a relationship that compounds rather than resets at every touchpoint.

Where It Applies

Products, platforms, places, communities, and worlds.

Playable Systems Design can be applied wherever organizations want participation to deepen across multiple interactions rather than disappear after a single campaign, transaction, event, or screen.

Entertainment & IP

Connect stories, games, creators, communities, products, and physical experiences around a shared audience relationship.

Brands & Loyalty

Move beyond points and promotions toward participation systems that recognize identity, progress, and contribution.

Physical + Digital

Make places, products, events, and digital experiences behave like parts of one connected system.

Where is your experience breaking apart?

If the pieces are valuable but disconnected, that is usually where the opportunity starts.
